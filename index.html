Synopsis

The narrative operates as both a precise tactical memoir and a philosophical manual for self-actualization. Tracking the trajectory of a life engineered from absolute minimalism, it challenges foundational paradigms regarding capital, destiny, and institutional change.

Born from five rupees and extraordinary resilience, this story reaches across continents to deliver a message of profound hope.

The Author

Francis Soundararajan

Author & Chaplain

Francis Soundararajan is a professional chaplain and clinical spiritual care practitioner with a background in pastoral ministry, education, and healthcare chaplaincy. He brings a cross-cultural perspective shaped by service in India and the United States. He completed multiple Clinical Pastoral Education (CPE) units, including a residency at Vanderbilt University Medical Center, where he served in ICU, pediatrics, mental health, and palliative care. His work centers on compassionate presence, meaning-making, and holistic spiritual care.

Production House

Jürgen John Publishing

Jürgen John is a retired engineering executive from Daimler-Benz, entrepreneur, mentor, world traveler, philanthropist, and founder of Hooklift. Together with his wife Uta, he has dedicated his life to encouraging people, building relationships across cultures, and supporting meaningful causes. What began as a chance meeting on a beach in Chennai evolved into a remarkable friendship that bridged East and West, eventually becoming an important part of the journey behind this book.
